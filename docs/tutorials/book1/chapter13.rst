.. _book1-ch13-early_stopping:

Early Stopping
--------------

Demonstrates early stopping: the training error keeps falling while the validation error eventually rises, so training should stop at the minimum of the validation error to avoid overfitting.

.. image:: /_static/screenshots/book1/ch13_early_stopping.webp
   :alt: Early Stopping demo window
   :width: 100%

**How to use it**

Use the slider to change the Noise Standard Deviation of the training points.

Click [Train] to train on the training points.

The training and validation performance indexes will be presented on the right.

You will notice that without early stopping the validation error will increase.

**Controls**

- Noise standard deviation (slider)
- Pause (button)

.. _book1-ch13-regularization:

Regularization
--------------

Shows regularization, which adds a penalty on the size of the weights to the performance index. The regularization ratio sets the trade-off between fitting the data and keeping the network smooth.

.. image:: /_static/screenshots/book1/ch13_regularization.webp
   :alt: Regularization demo window
   :width: 100%

**How to use it**

Click [Train] to train the network on the noisy data points.

Use the slide bars to choose the Regularization Ratio and the Noise Standard Deviation.

**Controls**

- Noise standard deviation (slider)
- Regularization Ratio (slider)
- Train (button)

**Source:** ``nndesigndemos/book1/chapter13/Regularization.py``

.. _book1-ch13-bayesian_regularization:

Bayesian Regularization
-----------------------

Demonstrates Bayesian regularization, which chooses the regularization parameters automatically from the data and reports the effective number of parameters the network is using.

.. image:: /_static/screenshots/book1/ch13_bayesian_regularization.webp
   :alt: Bayesian Regularization demo window
   :width: 100%

**How to use it**

Click [Train] to train the network on the noisy data points.

Use the slide bars to choose the Network Size, the Number of data points, the Noise Standard Deviation and the frequency of the function.

**Controls**

- Noise standard deviation (slider)
- # Hidden Neurons (slider)
- # Data Points (slider)
- Frequency (slider)
- Train (button)

.. _book1-ch13-early_stopping_regularization:

Early Stopping-Regularization
-----------------------------

Shows the link between early stopping and regularization: on a quadratic error surface the early-stopping trajectory passes through the same points as the minima of the regularized performance index for increasing regularization ratios.

.. image:: /_static/screenshots/book1/ch13_early_stopping_regularization.webp
   :alt: Early Stopping-Regularization demo window
   :width: 100%

**How to use it**

Click on the epoch slider to see the steepest descent trajectory.

Click on the ro slider to see the minimum of the regularized performance index.

**Controls**

- Epoch (slider)
- ro (slider)

**Source:** ``nndesigndemos/book1/chapter13/Early_stoppping_regularization.py``
