.. _book1-ch05-gram_schmidt:

Gram schmidt
------------

Illustrates Gram-Schmidt orthogonalization, which turns a set of independent vectors into an orthogonal set. Orthogonal bases make the vector-space calculations used later in the book much simpler.

.. image:: /_static/screenshots/book1/ch05_gram_schmidt.webp
   :alt: Gram schmidt demo window
   :width: 100%

**How to use it**

Click [Start] to begin.

Click twice in the top graph to create two vectorsto be orthogonalized. Then click [Compute] to see the orthogonal vectors.

Click [Start] again to repeat.

**Controls**

- Compute (button)
- Start (button)

.. _book1-ch05-reciprocal_basis:

Reciprocal basis
----------------

Shows how to expand a vector in terms of a basis that is not orthogonal: the reciprocal basis vectors give the expansion coefficients directly.

.. image:: /_static/screenshots/book1/ch05_reciprocal_basis.webp
   :alt: Reciprocal basis demo window
   :width: 100%

**How to use it**

Click on the plot to define the basis {v1, v2} and the vector x to be expanded in terms of {v1, v2}.

Click [Expand] to expand a new vector.

Click [Start] to define a new basis.

**Controls**

- Expand (button)
- Start (button)
